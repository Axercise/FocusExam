# FocusExam ambient audio

All three source sound pages were checked during preparation and identify their audio
as **CC0 1.0 Universal**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

| Local asset | Original sound | Creator | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| rain.mp3 | gentle rainfall.wav | rasunter255 | https://freesound.org/people/rasunter255/sounds/416227/ |
| ocean.mp3 | Ocean waves mono.mp3 | tec_studio | https://freesound.org/people/tec_studio/sounds/232186/ |
| noise.mp3 | White noise | theundecided | https://freesound.org/people/theundecided/sounds/165058/ |

The public HQ MP3 previews linked by these source pages were downloaded directly.
No authenticated original downloads or YouTube extraction were used.
Each local asset is an edited 59-second loop: first 60 seconds, one-second
tail/head crossfade, normalized to -20 LUFS, mono 32 kHz, MP3 96 kbps.

See licenses.json for source preview URLs, verification times, SHA-256 hashes,
processing records, and file sizes. The optional preparation script is
scripts/prepare-ambient-audio.mjs (requires a locally installed FFmpeg binary).
Files are served by this app; playback makes no request to Freesound.
